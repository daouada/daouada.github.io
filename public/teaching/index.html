Educational Initiatives

  • SmartSchoul2025 Entreprise classes — 2022-2025

This project was in collaboration with the SnT, the Ministry of National Education via SCRIPT, and the Lycée Edward Steichen à Clervaux (LESC), the project promotes early engagement with digital technologies and awareness of digital ethics.
